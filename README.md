# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Regular cards show verified, openly licensed in-game action photos when available, and otherwise use the on-field player illustration; player portraits are reserved for case hits. Searches use the player's full name, NFL/football terms, and team city, with a suffix-free name variant where applicable. The app only uses results with a matching player name, football-game metadata, and an accepted open license, and prioritizes files that explicitly identify an action or a player-named game matchup. Failed action photos are removed rather than replaced with a portrait. Card details link to the Commons source and show its photographer and license. Photo search requires an internet connection.
