# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Every card shows a player photo immediately: regular cards use an ESPN player photo until a verified, openly licensed game-action photo is found, while case-hit cards keep the player portrait. Searches use the player's full name, NFL/football terms, and team city, with a suffix-free name variant where applicable. The app only uses results with a matching player name, football-game metadata, and an accepted open license, and prioritizes files that explicitly identify an action or a player-named game matchup. If an action photo fails to load, the ESPN photo is restored. Card details link to the Commons source and show its photographer and license. Photo search requires an internet connection.
