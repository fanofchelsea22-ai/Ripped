# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Base, autograph, and numbered cards search Wikimedia Commons for openly licensed on-field action photos as they appear; case-hit cards are exempt and may keep their designed face/art treatment. Searches use the player's full name, NFL/football terms, and team city, with a suffix-free name variant where applicable. The app only uses results with a matching player name, football-game metadata, and an accepted open license, and prioritizes files that explicitly identify an action or a player-named game matchup. Temporary search failures are retried, and a failed image load falls back to another verified match; a player without a verified match keeps the original card artwork. Card details link to the source and show the photographer and license. Photo search requires an internet connection.
