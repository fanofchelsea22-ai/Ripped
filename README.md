# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Base, autograph, and numbered cards search Wikimedia Commons for openly licensed on-field action photos as they appear; case-hit cards are exempt and may keep their designed face/art treatment. The app only uses results with a matching player name, football-game metadata, and an accepted open license, and prioritizes files that explicitly identify an action or a player-named game matchup. Card details link to the source and show the photographer and license. Players without a verified match keep the original card artwork. Photo search requires an internet connection.
