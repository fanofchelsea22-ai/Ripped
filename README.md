# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Regular cards show verified, openly licensed in-game action photos when available, with a polished chrome frame and refractor sheen; otherwise they use the on-field player illustration. Player portraits are reserved for case hits. Searches use the player's full name, team city, and suffix-free name variant. The app prioritizes openly licensed photos with player/game details, while still verifying the player's name and excluding portraits and unrelated event photos. Failed action photos are removed rather than replaced with a portrait. Card details link to the Commons source and show its photographer and license. Photo search requires an internet connection.
