# Ripped
Open Sports card. Modern cards and sets from previous rip your cards and sell them at the store, or list them online. You can also grade them with the grading services.

The player catalog includes an ESPN NFL roster snapshot, including reserve and practice squad players. Keep `sunday-breaks.html` and `nfl-roster.js` together when serving the app.

Base, autograph, and numbered cards search Wikimedia Commons for openly licensed on-field action photos as they appear; case-hit cards are exempt and may keep their designed face/art treatment. Searches use the player's full name, NFL/football terms, and team city, with a suffix-free name variant where applicable. The app only uses results with a matching player name, football-game metadata, and an accepted open license, and prioritizes files that explicitly identify an action or a player-named game matchup. ESPN player photos display immediately and remain as a fallback when no verified action photo is available; verified Commons action photos replace them when found. Temporary search failures are retried, and a failed action-image load falls back to another verified match. Card details link to the Commons source and show its photographer and license. Photo search requires an internet connection.
